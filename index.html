Status

Your character sheet — name, class, and 5 stats plotted on the pentagon.

Available Points

Every stat and your point pool start at 0 — nothing is given for free. Tap the + next to a stat to spend points and grow it, and earn points by completing quests (+1), keeping your streak alive (+2), or adding a log entry (+1).

Rank badge

Get all 5 stats to 100 to rank up (E → SSS). Each rank-up clears the pentagon back to 0 so you can climb toward the next one — until SSS, which stays maxed for good. SS and above glow crimson, and a brief congratulations appears each time you rank up.

Wealth / Skills / Knowledge

Tap those names on the pentagon to open a log where you can add or delete your own entries. Adding one earns a point too.

Daily quest

Your checklist for the day. Tap a box to mark it complete and earn a point.

Routine

Open it from "Routine ›" on Status. Set different tasks for each day of the week, tick them off as you go — they reset automatically the next time that day comes around.

+ (new quest)

Create your own quests: set a target, category, and optionally a crimson Boss Quest. Name the category after a pentagon stat (Strength, Wealth, Skills, Knowledge, or Agility) and completing it feeds that stat directly instead of the general point pool — for Wealth, Skills or Knowledge it also adds an entry to that stat's log.

Running & Agility

Any quest with "run" or "jog" in its name automatically feeds Agility and the running chart — multiple running quests on the same day add together.

Growth Tree

A visual tree with clickable regions — tap a branch, trunk, or the roots for details.

Streak & History

Your running chart plus a full-year calendar — green for days you ran, red for days you didn't. Use ‹ › to browse other years. Keeping the streak alive earns bonus points.

Fail Daily Quest

Simulates missing your quests and triggers the penalty countdown.

Export / Import

Save your data as a file on the Streak page, or load it back in later.

Monthly Recap & Total Stats

Tap STRENGTH on the pentagon. A line chart of your combined stat total (last 14 days, drops to zero after each rank-up), plus quests done, days fully completed, distance run, points earned and your streaks for any month — use ‹ › to browse.

Sounds

Soft sound effects play on taps, quest completion, rank-ups, penalties and glitches. Lower your device volume to mute them.

Erase data

The trash icon at the top-left of Create Status wipes everything (all profiles) after a warning. It cannot be undone.

Install as an app

When this page is hosted on https or localhost, your browser shows an Install button. Installed, it appears as "System" with a dark icon.

Name + Age

These work like a login. Change either one to start a fresh character, or re-enter an old name and age to restore that profile.

Appearance

The circle in the top-right of Create Status switches the whole system between the blue "Darkness" look and the golden "Divine" look.
